FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official ICAR rank?

No. It is an unofficial statistical estimate.

What data is used?

The active Piscipedia historical dataset imported from the administrator's PDF.

Does category affect the result?

Only when the active dataset contains sufficient relevant category data. The supplied 2026 PDF provides score and predicted overall rank pairs, not category-specific fields.

Does domicile affect the result?

Only when supported by the imported data. The supplied PDF does not provide domicile-specific rank records.

Can I submit again?

Yes. Each submission is stored independently.
